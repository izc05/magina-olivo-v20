# RC1.2 Brand Asset Brief

**Status:** DESIGN BRIEF  
**Current leading candidate:** OleaTrack  
**Final brand:** pending Naming Gate

## 1. Required master assets

When Naming Gate closes, create:

1. horizontal wordmark;
2. stacked wordmark;
3. symbol-only mark;
4. Android adaptive launcher icon;
5. monochrome icon;
6. splash mark;
7. dark-background variant;
8. light-background variant;
9. Google Play feature graphic;
10. web favicon/app-icon family;
11. social/profile avatar;
12. onboarding/marketing hero image direction.

## 2. Symbol concept

Preferred conceptual ingredients:

- olive branch;
- one or two olives;
- optional subtle parcel/location/track reference;
- modern geometric simplification.

The symbol must remain recognizable at launcher-icon scale.

Avoid:
- detailed full olive tree;
- shields/crests;
- tractors in the logo;
- maps of Jaén/Spain;
- multiple tiny agricultural symbols;
- text inside the app icon.

## 3. Wordmark

Working candidate:
`OleaTrack`

Visual direction:
- modern sans-serif;
- strong “Olea” / “Track” distinction may be explored;
- no script type for the main brand;
- optional handwritten line only for marketing slogan, never the product name.

## 4. App icon

Preferred structure:

```text
dark olive rounded adaptive background
+
light simplified olive branch
+
one focused olive / tracking-location gesture
```

Requirements:
- works at 48px;
- safe adaptive-icon margins;
- no text;
- recognizable in monochrome where needed;
- distinct from generic olive-oil bottle brands.

## 5. Splash

Minimal:

- cream/light background or olive background;
- centered symbol;
- wordmark optional;
- no feature list;
- no loading messages unless technically needed.

## 6. Photography

Preferred:
- real olive groves;
- parcel geometry visible in landscape where possible;
- workers/machinery only when authentic;
- natural light;
- Mediterranean but not exclusively one province.

Avoid:
- obvious stock-photo poses;
- excessive warm filters;
- rustic nostalgia aesthetic;
- only Jaén-specific landmarks.

## 7. Illustration system

When photography is unsuitable:

- clean vector;
- simple shapes;
- olive/sage/cream palette;
- map/parcel cues;
- minimal shadows;
- no cartoon farmer mascot for core product.

## 8. Google Play assets

Future deliverables after final naming:

- 512×512 app icon;
- 1024×500 feature graphic;
- 6–8 phone screenshots;
- short description;
- full description;
- privacy/data-safety aligned visuals.

Screenshot story:

1. Controla tus fincas y parcelas
2. Planifica y registra trabajos
3. Gestiona cosecha y entregas
4. Escanea vales y facturas
5. Analiza costes y rentabilidad
6. Consulta tiempo y radar
7. Revisa histórico de campañas
8. Todo tu olivar, incluso sin cobertura

## 9. Marketing slogans — working set

Not approved final copy:

- Tu olivar, bajo control.
- Gestiona hoy, mejora mañana.
- Más control. Más rentabilidad.
- Tecnología al servicio del olivar.

## 10. Naming dependency

Do not export final production assets under `OleaTrack` until:

- trademark screen;
- store-name screen;
- domain screen;
- user approval;
- Naming Gate closed.

Until then, concept assets may use OleaTrack but must be labeled working/provisional.

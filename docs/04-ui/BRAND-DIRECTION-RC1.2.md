# RC1.2 Approved Visual Direction

**Status:** APPROVED DIRECTION  
**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Public name:** still pending Naming Gate  
**Current leading candidate:** OleaTrack

## 1. Visual character

The approved direction is:

- modern;
- olive-specific;
- premium but practical;
- clean and bright;
- natural without rustic clichés;
- suitable for outdoor field use;
- visually strong enough for Google Play and a later promotional website.

## 2. Brand language

Working visual language:

- olive branch / olive fruit;
- subtle parcel/map reference;
- olive green as primary accent;
- sage secondary;
- warm cream background;
- restrained earth accent;
- dark charcoal text;
- real olive-grove photography as supporting visual material.

Avoid:

- wood textures;
- vintage farm aesthetics;
- overly brown palettes;
- complex heraldic logos;
- decorative dashboards;
- generic all-crop iconography.

## 3. Working color direction

Provisional visual palette:

- Olive primary: around #556B2F
- Sage: around #8DA874
- Cream: around #F7F2E8
- Earth accent: around #C9A67A
- Dark text: around #2E2E2E

Exact production values are frozen later as design tokens after contrast testing.

## 4. Logo direction

Approved concept direction:

- a simple olive branch / olive fruit symbol;
- optional subtle map/parcel/location reference;
- readable at launcher-icon size;
- independent symbol + wordmark;
- suitable for monochrome and small-size use.

Do not freeze the final logo until the Naming Gate closes.

## 5. App icon direction

- rounded Android adaptive icon;
- strong dark olive background;
- simplified white/light olive branch;
- one focal olive/map detail;
- no small text;
- recognizable at 48 px.

## 6. Product UI direction

The approved visual reference should feel clearly more modern than the competitor reference.

Core screen qualities:

- large readable metrics;
- restrained cards;
- clear hierarchy;
- campaign context always visible;
- production/cost/profitability charts that are operational, not decorative;
- farm cover photography where useful;
- bottom navigation: Inicio · Mi Olivar · Registrar (+) · Calendario · Perfil.

## 8. Weather-responsive Home

The Home visual direction includes subtle weather-responsive atmosphere:

- sun glow;
- clouds;
- rain;
- fog;
- restrained wind/leaf movement.

Readability, reduced motion, battery and device performance always override decoration.

## 9. Status of "OleaTrack"

OleaTrack is the leading naming candidate for concept work only.

It is not yet the final official name and must not trigger a package-ID rename before the Naming Gate closes.

## 10. Implementation rule

Figma/design work may use the OleaTrack working mark for exploration, but production code must treat branding as replaceable configuration until the final brand is approved.

This direction is now the visual baseline for Phase 3 unless the product owner explicitly requests a change.
